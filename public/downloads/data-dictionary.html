COMMERCE OPERATIONS EXPLORER

Data dictionary and business definitions

Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset. Currency: BRL. Timestamps retain source wall-clock values.

Metrics and eligibility

  • Delivered merchandise value: item prices for delivered orders, excluding freight.
  • Average order value: delivered merchandise value divided by delivered-order count.
  • Late delivery: actual calendar date after estimated calendar date; denominator excludes missing dates and undelivered orders.
  • Latest review: answer timestamp, then creation timestamp, then source-row key, in descending order.
  • Period filters always refer to purchase date. Geography belongs to the order destination, not an assumed permanent customer address.
